Creating and adding a calibration

The first step of the calibration is its creation. You can also use this procedure to add a new calibration.

To create a calibration:

  1. Open the Calibration panel:

    • In the Device manager or Areas panel, in the top left corner, click and click the Calibration tile.
    • Go to Location → Management and click the Calibration tile.

  2. In the top right corner of the panel, click Add calibration.

  3. Into the Calibration name field, type the name of your calibration.

    Note

    The calibration name is set only once. After you create the calibration, you will not be able to change it.

  4. Click Create new.
