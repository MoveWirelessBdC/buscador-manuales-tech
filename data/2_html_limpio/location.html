Location Statistics

The Location Statistics provide information about the user behaviour at your venue and the corresponding area. To view them, go to the Reporting > Location section. It consists of two tiles: Venue and Area.

Venue

At the top of the statistics window you can choose and then display a specified time frame(from-to). To define it, select the time range in the Time Picker located at the top of the Statistics panel. Then, click Show to confirm your selection.

Overview

Passers-By

The Passers-By table displays the number of unique users who have been situated within your WiFi network's reach but have not been classified as spotted inside.

Visitors

The Visitors table shows the number of unique users who are situated within the network's reach. They differ from the Passers-By by their location, which is known.

Passers-By and Visitors

The Passers-By and Visitors chart presents the total number of users in the chosen time frame. To view the specifics for each day hover your mouse over the appropriate area.

Rush Hours

The Rush Hours section provides a chart which presents the distribution of hours when the user traffic is at a peak. It is a chart which displays the average number of users per hour, in colours; the higher the number of Logged-In Users, the darker the shade of the given field is. It is a visualisation of daytime traffic at your venue, which allows you to identify the time of day when the user congestion is at its peak, and adequately plan the customer service deployment.

To view the number of users at a specific time, hover your mouse over the appropriate area.

New and Returning Visitors

The New and Returning Visitors bar chart shows the total number of new and returning located users. It may be presented per hour, day, and week. To view the exact number, hover your mouse over the desired bar.

You can also filter this graph by the type of users. To do this, select the preferred user type from the legend located at the bottom of the table.

Dwell Time Distribution of Visitors

The Dwell Time Distribution of Visitors bar chart displays the distribution of the number of sessions by their duration.

The Average Dwell Time for Visitors section presents the average duration of all visitor sessions. It is calculated for Located Visitors as a total duration of all sessions divided by the total number of sessions.

The Total Dwell Time for Visitors section presents the total duration of all visitor sessions. It is calculated as the sum of all Visitors’ sessions.

Traffic Map

The Traffic Map table shows congestion of Visitors at your Venue.

You can filter the table’s results by setting the area and time granularity. To do this, click the Filter icon in the top right corner of the table and select the desired settings. At the bottom of the Traffic Map you can use the Play button to see an animation of the density of the chosen time frame.

User Traffic

In the User Traffic bar chart, you can view the number of users broken by who entered and exited the Venue, displayed per day or week. The Area Picker also works as a Heat-Map.

Traffic per Camera

The Traffic per Camera table shows the list of all devices by their IDs with the number of entries and exits that have been registered.

Areas

The Area section consists of three tabs which you can find at the top of the statistics window: the Area, the Flow Diagram, and Ranking.

Area

You can choose and then display a specified time frame(from-to). To define it, select the time range in the Time Picker located at the top of the Statistics panel. Then, click Show to confirm your selection.

Passers-By

The Passers-By table displays the number of unique users who have been situated within your WiFi network's reach but have not been classified as spotted inside.

Visitors

The Visitors table shows the number of unique users who are situated within the network's reach. They differ from the Passers-By by their location, which is known and the user is classified as present in the area.

Passers-By and Visitors

The Passers-By and Visitors chart presents the total number of users in the chosen time frame. To view the specifics for each day hover your mouse over the appropriate area.

Rush Hours

The Rush Hours section provides a chart which presents the distribution of hours when the user traffic is at a peak. It is a chart which displays the average number of users per hour, in colours; the higher the number of Logged-In Users, the darker the shade of the given field is. It is a visualisation of daytime traffic at your venue, which allows you to identify the time of day when the user congestion is at its peak, and adequately plan the customer service deployment.

To view the number of users at a specific time, hover your mouse over the appropriate area.

Flow Diagram

The Flow diagram shows you how your users move between areas.

Note: You can use the Min. number of users to filter the data so that it can remove insignificant transitions.

Ranking

The Ranking section allows you to compare areas between different metrics. The Sort option in the table allows you to easily see which areas have the most Passers-By and Visitors and where the highest density appears.

Map of Areas

This map can be displayed for a specified time frame and metric type (Visitors and Passers-By by count and density). To define it, select the time range in the Time Picker located at the top of the Areas window, and choose the metric type from the Metric Type dropdown list. Then, click Show to confirm your selection.

Note: The Area Picker also works as a Heat-Map.

Areas Ranking

Below the Map of Areas, you can find a list of the area details. You can filter it, by selecting options from the toggle menu above the list in the top right corner(Area Name, Area Floor, Select All), or use the search field to find the preferred area.
