Requesting a Top-Up (for Clients)

If your Licence has used up all of its dedicated data transfer, you can request an additional top-up.

Such top-ups are derived from Data Transfer Packages which should be previously created by your Representative. So before you request a top-up, ensure they have them configured.

To request a top-up, go to the Licence Manager, and choose the desired Organisation. from the Organisation tree on the left.

  1. In the top right corner of the desired Licence panel, click the More option and select Request a Top-Up from the dropdown menu.

  2. Now, in the new window, select the preferred Data Transfer Package from the Data Transfer Package dropdown list. When you select a Package, you can preview its details below the dropdown list.

    After choosing the desired Package, click Send Request in the top right corner of the window to send the request to your Representative.

Previewing Historical Requests

You can preview your requests and check their status by clicking the Show Requests button on the right side of the Licences workspace.

Three statuses represent the state of requests:

  • Accepted means that your request has been approved and the data transfer of the Licence has been topped up with the requested amount of data.
  • Pending means that your request still awaits resolution.
  • Rejected means that your request has been declined. For more information, you should contact your Representative.
