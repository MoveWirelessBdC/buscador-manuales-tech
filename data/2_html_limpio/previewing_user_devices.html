Previewing End-User Devices

When you have correctly configured your Venue in the Location mode, you can preview the real-time position of end-user devices on Venue maps.

To do this, select the Dashboard > Behaviour section from the main menu, and go to the Where Are Your Users Now? sub-section where you can find two tables: Device Mapper and Traffic Map.

Device Mapper

The Device Mapper table displays Visitors' position at your Venue live.

You can search through the devices located at your Venue by clicking the List icon in the top right corner of the table and providing a MAC address or a device name. You may also click on a specific device to see its details and localise it on the map.

Traffic Map

The Traffic Map table shows live congestion of Visitors at your Venue.

You can filter the table’s results by the User type (Located or Logged In) and set the area granularity. To do this, click the Filter icon in the top right corner of the table and select the desired settings.
