Creating and Editing a Page Flow

To know more about Page Flows, read the Page Flow Overview.

Creating a Page Flow

To create a new Page Flow:

  1. Go to the User Enrollment → User Journey section and click the Page Flow tile.

  2. In the Page Flow Manager, click + in the bottom right corner.

  3. In the Settings panel, type the Flow name. In the top right corner, click Next.

  4. Add and configure the nodes. Afterwards, in the upper-right corner, click Save.

Editing an Existing Page Flow

  1. Go to the User Enrollment → User Journey section and click the Page Flow tile.

  2. In the Page Flow Manager, you may see your flow in the Own category. Click the three dots on the bottom right corner of the tile to access the drop-down menu and then, select Edit.

    NOTE: There are two types of Page Flows that may be seen in the Page Flow Manager: Own and Inherited. The Own Page Flows were created by your organisation. The Inherited Flows are the ones which were created in the multi-level organisation to which your organisation belongs.

  3. At this stage you may add and delete nodes according to your preferences. After you have finished, click Save in the upper right-hand corner.

Copying a Page Flow

  1. In the Page Flow Manager, select a Flow which you want to edit. Click the three dots on the bottom of its right corner and click Edit.

  2. Type the name of the copied Page Flow and select Copy Options. You may choose from one of two options: you can either copy all elements used within the Page Flow (including Portals and Forms), or you can save the copy as a draft. Please note that you can only select one option.

    You can also leave this section unselected. In this way, you will copy the Page Flow by itself, without its additional components.

    Then, click Save.

  3. You can now see the copied Flow in the Own category.

Note: You can also copy Page Flows inherited from the organisation to which you belong. Choose the desired Page Flow from the Inherited section, and recreate the steps 1-3. The copied Page Flow will then be saved in the Own section.
