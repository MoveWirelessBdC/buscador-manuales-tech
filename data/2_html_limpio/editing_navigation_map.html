Editing navigation map

If you've mistakenly assigned some premises to the wrong area, positioned stairs or toilets in the wrong place, or you just want to modify the navigation line, read this chapter to learn how to do it.

Changing an outline of marked areas

To change the outline of the marked area:

  1. Go to Location Management → Navigation.

  2. Next to the name of your venue, click Map.

  3. On the left side of the screen, on the taskbar, click .

  4. Find the outline area you want to modify, on the dashed line, click on the dot and drag it to a new position.

  5. Next to , click Save.

  6. On the right side of the screen, click Apply changes.

Changing an assignment of an area to a place

To change an assignment of an area to a place:

  1. Go to Location Management → Navigation.

  2. Next to the name of your venue, click Map.

  3. Right-click the marked area and from the list, select Bind area to premises.

  4. In the Available premises list, change the name of the premises for the premises you want to locate there.

  5. Click Save.

  6. On the right side of the screen, click Apply changes.

Changing placement of services

To change the placement of stairs, lifts, escalators, cashpoints, and toilets:

  1. Go to Location Management → Navigation.

  2. Next to the name of your venue, click Map.

  3. On the left side of the screen, on the taskbar, click .

  4. On the map, click the object you want to move and place it in the new spot.

    Note

    In case of changing the position of stairs, escalators, and lifts already connected to the navigation line, the connection between floors is not deleted, so do not move them far from their original place. If you want to change their position considerably, delete the connection and create a new one.

  5. Next to , click Save

  6. Change or add a navigation line so that it reaches the newly placed object.

  7. On the right side of the screen, click Apply changes.

Removing marking of an area, stairs, lifts, escalators, and services

To remove marking of an area, stairs, lifts, escalators, cash machines, and toilets:

  1. Go to Location Management → Navigation.

  2. Next to the name of your venue, click Map.

  3. On the left side of the screen, on the taskbar, click .

  4. Click the object you want to remove, and then next to the icon , click Save.

  5. On the right side of the screen, click Apply changes.

Changing navigation lines

To change navigation lines:

  1. Go to Location Management → Navigation.

  2. Click Edit venue map.

  3. Click the icon.

  4. On the navigation line, click the white dot, and change its placement.

  5. Next to the icon, click Finish.

  6. On the right side of the screen, click Apply changes.
