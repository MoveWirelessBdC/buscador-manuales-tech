Importing a venue

Read this procedure to learn how to import venue settings to the platform.

Note

To import a venue you must have a venue created.

To import a venue:

  1. Go to Location Management → Venue administration and then to the Venue import/export tab.

  2. In the Venue import section, click Import and select a venue file.

  3. Click Delete, and then to confirm, click OK.
