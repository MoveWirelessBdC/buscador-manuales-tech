Release Version: 23.08.18__stable__0.101.0

Release Date: 18/08/2023

New Features

  • We have integrated a new messaging vendor into our Sending Centre, partnering with TapTalk to empower users with WhatsApp messaging. The integration allows utilising WhatsApp for WiFi network logins, expanding our platform's capabilities.

Updates

System Updates

  • We are in the process of removing outdated features:
    • Removed deprecated Access Methods (PMS and Pay to access)
  • Morphia has been eliminated from our dependencies.
  • The MongoDB driver version has been upgraded.
  • We have enhanced the performance of our product:
    • Improved cache optimization for the Analytics module: Now, only modified entities are dumped into databases, and the dumping interval has been extended.
    • Implemented caching for TemporaryRadiusUser entities, which represent WiFi users during a Radius session.
    • Introduced caching for multimedia resources in the Captive Portal functionality and Whitelabeling modules, resulting in reduced MongoDB workload and minimised transfers within the cluster.
