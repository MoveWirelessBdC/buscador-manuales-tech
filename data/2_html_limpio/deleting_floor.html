Deleting a floor

You may need to modify the structure of a venue by deleting a floor. Read this chapter to learn how to do it.

Note

You can only remove floors with no access and calibration points.

To delete a floor:

  1. Go to Location management→ Management and then click the Venue map tile.

  2. In the Map panel, in the right upper corner, for the floor number you want to delete, click the button. Click OK.
