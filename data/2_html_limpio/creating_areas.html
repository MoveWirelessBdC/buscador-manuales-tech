Dividing the venue into areas (Optional)

It is possible to divide a venue into smaller areas in order to get statistics for each created area and create location-based marketing campaigns.

To divide the venue into areas:

  1. Open the Areas panel:

    • In the Device manager panel, in the top left corner, click the and then click the Areas tile.
    • Go to Location → Management and click the Areas tile.

  2. In the Area panel, in the right upper corner, select the floor which you want to divide into areas.

  3. Click the icon and draw the area:

    a. To start drawing, click on a spot in the plan.
    b. Continue drawing by clicking on consecutive spots in the plan. When the area is closed, the Area editor window opens.

  4. In the Area editor window, enter the area name and description.

  5. Click Save.

What to do next:

  • If you need precise data, then you need to perform calibration. Start with creating calibration.
  • If you do not want to go through the process of calibration and you need only very rough data, then configure the engine.
