Editing a calibration

You may need to make some corrections in the placement of the calibration points; read this section to learn how to do this.

Note

The changes are going to be applied within 10 minutes from saving.

Moving a calibration point

To move a calibration point:

  1. Go to Location management→ Management and then click the Calibration tab.

  2. Click , and on the venue plan, drag a marker point of the calibration point to a new place.

    Tip

    You can move only those points that are not already calibrated on any channel band.

  3. Next to the button, click Save.

Deleting a calibration point

To delete a calibration point:

  1. Go to Location management→ Management and then click the Calibration tab.

  2. Click , and on the venue plan, click the calibration point you want to delete.

  3. Next to the button, click Save.
