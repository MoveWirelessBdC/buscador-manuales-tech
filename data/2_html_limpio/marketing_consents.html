Creating, Editing and Deleting a Marketing Category

If you would like to reach your users with marketing offers, you may be required by law to first obtain from them permission to send these offers. To acquire permission, configure the Marketing Categories on your Portals or login pages.

Creating a Marketing Category

  1. Go to the User Enrollment → User Agreements section and select the Marketing Categories option.

  2. In the bottom right corner, click the + button.

  3. In the first step:

    • Type the Marketing Category’s name into the Marketing Category name field
    • From the Offer frequency dropdown list, select how often the document should be displayed to the users. For this instruction, it is Once.

    Note: only when users give their consent to receive offers, can these offers be sent to them.

    After you have finished providing these details, click Next in the top right corner of the window.

  4. In the following step, compose the text of the Marketing Category.

  5. In the last step, you can review the summary of selected settings and preview the Marketing Category by clicking the Preview Marketing Category button.

  6. The document has now been created as a Draft.

    Note: You should note that you must first publish the Marketing Category to add it to the Portal. To learn how to publish User Agreements, read the Publishing a User Agreement Document and Adding It to the Portal chapter. chapter.

Editing a Marketing Category

To edit a Marketing Category, click the More option on the chosen Category, and select Edit from the dropdown menu. Then, edit the Category according to your preferences, and save it to confirm changes.

You can view all the changes in the Versions section. To know more about Versions, read the Previewing User Agreement Versions chapter.

Deleting a Marketing Category

To delete a Marketing Category, click the More option on the chosen Category, and select Delete from the dropdown menu. Then, confirm deleting the chosen Category in the pop-up box.
