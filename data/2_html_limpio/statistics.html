Location Statistics

The Location Statistics provide information about the user behaviour at your venue. To view them, go to the Reporting > Behaviour section, and click the Location Statistics tile.

The Location Statistics consist of three tabs which you can find at the top of the statistics window: the Overview, the Rush Hour, and the User Behaviour. They can be displayed for a specified time frame and granularity (hour, day, or week). To define it, select the time range in the Time Picker located at the top of the Statistics panel, and choose the granularity from the Granularity dropdown list. Then, click Show to confirm your selection.

Overview

Visible

The Visible table displays the number of unique users who have been situated within your WiFi network's reach. Their devices must have been spotted by access points at least once within the designated period, but their location may or may not be known.

Located

The Located table shows the number of unique users who are situated within the network's reach. They differ from the Visible Users by their location, which is known.

Visible and Located Users

The Visible and Located Users bar chart presents the total number of Visible and Located Users. These data are displayed as a total number, broken by these two types. It may be shown per hour, day, and week.

Min. and Max. End-User Count

The Min. and Max. End-User Count bar chart shows the minimum and maximum number of all user types.

Ratio Located / Visible

The Ratio Located / Visible chart displays the ratio of the number of users located and visible, calculated in %.

Rush Hours

The Rush Hours section provides the graph which presents the distribution of hours when the user traffic is at a peak. It is a heat map which displays the average number of users per hour, in colours; the higher the number of Logged-In Users, the darker the shade of the given field is. It is a visualisation of daytime traffic at your venue, which allows you to identify the time of day when the user congestion is at its peak, and adequately plan the customer service deployment.

To view the number of users at a specific time, hover your mouse over the appropriate area.

User Behaviour

New and Returning Located Users

The New and Returning Located Users bar chart shows the total number of new and returning located users. It may be presented per hour, day, and week. To view the exact number, hover your mouse over the desired bar.

You can also filter this graph by the type of users. To do this, select the preferred user type from the legend located in the top right corner of the table.

Dwell Time Distribution of Located Users

The Dwell Time Distribution of Located Users bar chart displays the distribution of the number of sessions by their duration.

Average Dwell Time for Located Users

The Average Dwell Time for Located Users doughnut chart presents the average duration of all user sessions. It is calculated for Located Users as a total duration of all sessions divided by the total number of sessions.
