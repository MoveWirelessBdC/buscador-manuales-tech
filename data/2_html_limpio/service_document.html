Creating, Editing and Deleting a Service Document

The Platform allows you to create various Service Documents, such as the Terms of Use, and the Privacy Policy. These can be placed in the Portals and must be accepted by users before they log in to the WiFi.

Creating a Service Document

  1. Go to the User Enrollment → User Agreements section and select the Service Documents option.

  2. In the bottom right corner, click the + button.

  3. In the first step, you can add a new Service Document Group:

    • Type the Document title into the Service Document Group field (required)
    • Add the Document's content into the Content of Service Document input field, and style it according to your preferences

    After you have finished providing these details, click Next in the top right corner of the window.

  4. [Optional] In the second step, you can add supporting documents to the group. These will be displayed on the devices as the Service Document’s expandable section. Click the Add Document button to add a new document.

    Then, in the pop-up box:

    • Enter the Document’s name into the Document Name field (required)
    • Add the content of the Document into the Content of Document field

    To confirm adding the supporting document, click Save in the bottom right corner of the pop-up box.

    The supporting document has now been added to the Service Document Group. To continue to the next step, click Next in the top right corner of the window.

  5. [Optional] In the third step, you can define the text displayed next to the consent checkbox.

    You can also specify the checkbox selection settings by turning the bottom switches ON and OFF according to your preferences:

    • The Consent By Default option makes the consent checkbox selected by default
    • The Consent Changes Allowed option enables users to change their consents in their GDPR panel
    • The Consent Required option requires users to select the consent checkbox if they want to proceed further and gain internet access

  6. In the last step, you can review the summary of selected settings and preview the Service Document by clicking the Preview Service Document button.

    To finish creating the Service Document, click Save in the top right corner of the window.

  7. The document has now been created as a Draft.

    Note: You should note that in order to add the Service Document to the Portal, you must first publish it. To learn how to publish User Agreements, read the Publishing a User Agreement Document and Adding It to the Portal chapter.

Editing a Service Document

To edit a Service Document, click the More option on the Service Document of your choice, and select Edit from the dropdown menu. Then, edit the Document according to your preferences, and save it to confirm changes.

Note: You can preview all the changes in the Versions section. To know more about Versions, read the Previewing User Agreement Versions chapter.

Deleting a Service Document

To delete a Service Document, click the More option on the Service Document of your choice, and select Delete from the dropdown menu. Then, confirm deleting the Service Document in the pop-up box.
