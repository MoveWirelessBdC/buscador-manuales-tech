Marketing Statistics

The Marketing Data statistics provide you details on marketing agreements and marketing categories which are the most preferred by your WiFi users. To access them, go to the Reporting -> Statistics section and select Marketing Data from the upper bar.

Note: The table loading time depends on several factors, including selected date range, amount of data which must be aggregated, or your internet connection speed. The average display time should be less than 5 min, and should not exceed 10 min. In case it is higher, try reloading the page, or change the date range. If the data is still not displayed, contact your administrator.

To ensure the best system performance, part of the data is stored in probabilistic structures, therefore the expected error in relation to real data can be up to 0.1%.

These data can be displayed for a specified time frame which you can define by selecting the time range in the Time Picker at the top of the Statistics panel. Then, click Show to confirm your selection.

The What Do You Know About Marketing Agreements? Section

Marketing Agreements over Time

The Marketing Agreements over Time bar chart shows the number of users broken by those who have, and have not agreed to marketing options, such as receiving special offers by text messages or emails. The data can be displayed per hour, day or week. You can select the desired time settings from the dropdown list in the upper right corner of the table.

Marketing Agreements

The Marketing Agreements doughnut chart displays the percentage breakdown of users who have, and have not agreed to marketing options.

The Which Marketing Category Is Selected Most Often? Section

Marketing Categories

The Marketing Categories doughnut chart shows the percentage breakdown of marketing categories to which users have agreed. You can display the exact numbers by hovering your mouse over the part that interests you.

Marketing Agreements per Category

The Marketing Agreements per Category table presents the detailed breakdown of the marketing categories broken along with the number of users who agreed and did not agree to them.

Exporting the Data

All the graphs can be exported as CSV files. To download them, click the Export to CSV icon in the top right corner of the table and save the file on your computer.
