Location Notes

  • For the Ruckus with ZoneDirector, the recommended version is >= 9.13 (versions below 9.13 use LBS 2.1 and may cause additional issues);
  • For the Ruckus with SmartZone (VSZ, VSCG, SC, SCG....), the recommended version is >= 3.4 (versions below 3.4 use LBS 2.1, and may cause additional issues);
  • The Aruba Instant device is not recommended for large venues + Presence;
  • The ArubaOs (with controller), the recommended version is >= 6.4.2;
  • To correctly configure Huawei AC for location, turn the STA Location switch ON in the device's user interface;

  • To correctly configure Huawei Agile for location, turn the Information Upload switch ON in the device's user interface.
