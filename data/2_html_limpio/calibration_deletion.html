Deleting a calibration

This procedure deletes a calibration and everything you have done regarding it, that is, the placement of the calibration points and the physical calibration.

Note

You can delete only those calibrations which are not used.

To delete a calibration:

  1. Go to Location Management → Venue administration and then to the Advanced options tab.

  2. In the Calibration deletion panel:

    • From the Venue list, select the name of your venue.

    • From the Calibration name list, select the name of the calibration you want to delete.

  3. Click Delete, and click OK to confirm.
