Managing Client Requests (for Representatives)

As a Representative, you can manage client requests by accepting or rejecting them.

To do this, go to the Licence Manager and select the desired Organisation from the Organisation tree on the left. Then, on the right side of the upper bar, click the Show Requests button.

Then, in the Requests window, you can preview requests received from your Clients and issued for your Licences, had you been appointed any:

  • Select the Sent tab to view the requests sent for your Licences.

    Three statuses represent the state of requests:

    • Accepted means that your request has been approved and the data transfer of the Licence has been topped up with the requested amount of data.
    • Pending means that your request still awaits resolution.
    • Rejected means that your request has been declined. For more information, you should contact your Representative.

  • Select the Received tab to preview your Clients' pending requests.

Accepting and Rejecting a Client’s Request

  1. To accept or reject a Client’s request, go to the Received tab to preview all the requests and their details by moving the slider to the right.

  2. Next, find a request which you would like to resolve on the request list. Then, go to the Action column at the right end of the request row and click the More option.

    Then, select the desired action - Accept or Reject - from the dropdown menu:

    • By selecting Accept, you approve the Client’s request for a top-up, and a Licence is automatically topped up with the requested amount of data.
    • By choosing Reject, you decline the request for an additional data transfer.
