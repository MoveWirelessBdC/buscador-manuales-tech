Editing, Copying, and Removing Forms

Editing a Form

  1. In the Form Manager, click the More option and select Edit from the dropdown menu.

  2. In the Form Editor, edit the Form according to your preferences. In the upper right corner, click Save to confirm your changes.

Copying a Form

  1. In the Form Manager, click the More option and select Copy from the dropdown menu.

  2. Type the name of the copied Form. In the upper right corner, click Save to confirm.

  3. The copy of the Form has now been created. You can find it in the Form Manager.

Removing a Form

  1. In the Form Manager, click the More option and select Remove from the dropdown menu.

  2. In the pop-up box, click the Remove button to confirm deleting the Form. If you would like to keep it, select Keep.

Note: It is impossible to remove a Form which is being used in a Portal. To remove it, you must first remove the desired Form from the Portal. Then, follow the steps 1-2. To learn how to remove a Form from a Portal, read the Adding, Editing and Removing Forms From a Portal chapter.
