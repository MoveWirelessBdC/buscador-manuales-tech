Heat Map

The Heat Map graph displays the congestion of devices and indicates which places are visited most often by your clients. To view this map, go to the Reporting → Behaviour section and click the Heat Map tile.

Note: For the Heat Map to work correctly, you should ensure that: - all of the Access Points are set up and positioned across your venue, - you have uploaded the map of your venue to the platform and marked the APs in it.

In the upper right corner of the venue panel, you can choose the desired floor. You can also view the heat map in real-time or in the historical mode; to select the preferred one, go to the right panel and choose the desired mode from the top tabs.

To identify the congestion of devices, you may analyse the graph using a legend located in the bottom right corner of the window, which indicates the number of devices per 100 square meters.
