Editing premises

This chapter will help you change the name of a category of premises and delete a category.

Changing the name of a category

To change category name:

  1. Go to Location Management → Navigation.
  2. Next to the name of your venue, click Premises categories.
  3. In the Actions column, next to the name of the category you want to edit, click .
  4. Change the name and click Save.

Deleting a category

To delete a category:

  1. Go to Location Management → Navigation.
  2. Next to the name of your venue, click Premises categories.
  3. In the Actions column, next to the name of the category you want to edit, click .
  4. To confirm, click Delete.
