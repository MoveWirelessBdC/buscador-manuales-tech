Adding access points

The next step in the configuration of the Presence mode is the addition of access points.

To add access points:

  1. Go to Location Management → Management and then click the List of devices tile.

  2. Click Add device.

  3. In the Add access point window, into the Device's MAC address field, type a MAC address of the access point. Click Add.

  4. Repeat this procedure for all access points in your venue.
