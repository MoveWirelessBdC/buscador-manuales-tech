Creating and Managing a Marketing Campaign

Creating a Marketing Campaign

  1. Go to the Marketing → Campaigns section, and click + in the bottom right corner.

  2. Now, select the desired type of campaign by clicking on the appropriate tile. Then, follow the configuration steps in the Campaign Editor.

    Note: For this instruction, the Time-Limited campaign has been selected.

  3. In the Campaign Overview step, type the name of the campaign in the Name field, and enter the campaign description into the Description field. (The description is not displayed to end-users.) Then, in the upper right corner, click Next to proceed to the next step.

  4. In the Target Group step, select the desired target group by clicking on the appropriate tile. If you have no target group yet, you can create a new one. To learn how to do this, read the Creating a Target Group chapter.

  5. In the Marketing Categories step, you can choose the marketing categories by which the selected group should be targeted. Then, click Next to continue to the next step.

  6. In the Contact Methods step, you can choose the contact methods to reach your users. By default, both SMS and email are selected. To deactivate a contact method, turn the switch next to it OFF.

    You can also limit the number of text messages and/or emails which you would like to send to the users, and restrict the dispatch to phone numbers and/or email addresses which have been verified by the users.

    Next, determine the number of users to whom you would like to send the message in the Number of SMS and/or Number of Emails fields. Then, click Next to continue to the next step.

    Tip: The contact method at the top of the list is the primary one. You can manage the priority of these methods by dragging and dropping them according to your preferences.

  7. In the Message step, you can compose the message which you would like to send to your users. Depending on your selection in the previous step, this may be an SMS, an email, or both. Type the text in the Message Content field. Then, enter a phone number to which you would like to send a test SMS into the Phone Number field.

    Tip: To personalise the message, you may choose from the range of tags enlisted in the Tags column. Tags serve as data points for platform users and are replaced by the system with specific values. For instance, the $firstName tag serves as a data point for a user's first name. It is replaced in the final content (SMS, Email) with the user's first name. If the tag cannot find a proper value, it displays a default value which was previously set up in the system. To learn more about tags, read the Configuring Tag Settings chapter.

    After you have finished composing the message, click Next in the top right corner to proceed further.

  8. In the Delivery Schedule step, set the campaign delivery details and click Next in the top right corner.

  9. In the Summary step, you can review the campaign details. To finish the configuration process and schedule the campaign, click Send Campaign in the top right corner.

  10. The campaign is now set to be dispatched at the designated time.

Managing a Marketing Campaign

Once the campaign is dispatched, you can preview its status in the Campaign List. You can also pause it, remove it, and preview its results.
