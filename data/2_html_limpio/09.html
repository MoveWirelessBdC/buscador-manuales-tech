What’s New in September 2020

Email Customisation

The new Email Customisation feature allows you to adjust email messages which are generated by the Platform at specific circumstances and sent to designated recipients. Those email messages include user administration, e.g. password reset messages, and licence notifications.

You can access this feature by going to the Portal Management > Sending Centre section in the main menu and selecting the Email Customisation tile. At the top, you can find two tabs: Content, and Format. The Content tab enables you to modify texts of each email message on the list, and in the Format tab, you can adjust visual templates for those messages.

To learn how to do this, go to the User Guide and follow the instructions in the Email Customisation chapter.

Printing User Accounts Details

You can now print User Account details in the form of tokens and customise their look according to your preferences. To do this, select the Display Account Print View from the More option’s dropdown menu.

Note: Customising the print view requires basic knowledge of HTML and CSS.

New Login Methods over Time Chart

We have also added a new Login Methods over Time chart to the WiFi Users statistics which shows the number of users broken by login methods that they have chosen to log in to your network, displayed per selected granularity.
