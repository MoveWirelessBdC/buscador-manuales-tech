Domain Customisation

The Domain Customisation feature allows you to customise the platform's graphical interface, and adjust it to your company's standards. It is a useful on-prem solution for companies that use the platform on their domains.

Creating, Editing, and Removing a Domain Customisation

Creating a Domain Customisation

  1. Go to the Portal Management → Domain Customisation section, and click + in the bottom right corner to create a new customisation.

  2. In the Basic Information window, enter the domain details:

    • Into the Domain field, type the domain’s address (required),
    • Into the Site Title field, type the domain’s title,
    • In the Google Site Verification Tag, enter the appropriate verification tag.

    Then, click Save in the top right corner to complete creating the customisation.

Editing a Domain Customisation

To edit the Domain Customisation, click the More option in the top right corner of the desired domain, and select Edit from the dropdown menu. Then, amend the details and click Save to confirm the changes (see pt 2 of the Creating a Domain Customisation subchapter).

Removing a Domain Customisation

To remove the Domain Customisation, click the More option in the top right corner of the desired domain, and select Remove from the dropdown menu.

Branding Customisation

The Branding option allows you to set up your company’s logos and pictures in the designated places across the platform. These are the site logo, the site image, the top bar logo, the favicon, and the error page image. Each of these pictures is indicated during the customisation process.

The Branding editor consists of two steps, each of which is optional and may be skipped by clicking Skip This Step in the upper right corner.

  1. To begin the branding customisation, click the Pen icon on the left in the Branding section of the desired domain.

  2. In the Login Site step, you may set up a site image and a logo which the platform users can see while logging in to the platform.

    To upload the pictures, drag and drop the appropriate files into the designated areas, or click on the drop areas and select the desired files from your computer.

    In case you need to replace the uploaded picture, click on the More option which appears next to it, and select Edit from the dropdown list. Then, re-upload the preferred image.

    To remove the picture, select Remove from the dropdown list.

    When you have finished setting up the pictures, click Next in the top right corner to proceed to the next step.

  3. In the Top Bar Logo step, you may set up the image which appears on the top navigation bar. Then, click Next to proceed further.

  4. The Favicon step allows you to customise the icon located in the browser’s tab. After you have set it up, click Next in the top right corner to proceed further.

  5. The Error Page step enables setting up the image which appears on pages in the event of error. After having set it up, click Save in the top right corner to conclude the brand customisation process.

Theme Customisation

The Theme customisation allows you to manage the platform colour pallette and adjust it according to your brand identification. The Theme editor consists of two steps, both of which are optional and may be skipped by clicking Skip This Step in the upper right corner.

  1. In the Theme section, click the Pen icon on the right to begin to customise the theme.

  2. In the Basic Palette step, you can change the five basic colours:

    • The Background colour,
    • The Primary Colour which is the dominant colour on the platform,
    • The Secondary Colour which distinguishes those parts of the platform that trigger actions, for instance, buttons,
    • The Success Colour which is applied in notifications of successful completion of actions,
    • The Warning Colour which appears in all error notifications.

    When you have set up the desired colours, click Next in the top right corner to proceed to the next step.

  3. The Editors Palette step allows you to change the colours which are applied to the top bars of the application editors, and the headers and nodes in the Flow Editor.

    Note: By changing these colours, you also change the palette of illustrations that are included in the features' components.

  4. To complete the customisation process, click Save in the top right corner.

GDPR Customisation

The GDPR customisation allows you to select the Organisation from which you would like to use the SMS, Email, and Social Media configuration for the GDPR panel. You can also manage the Login Types available for your WiFi users for selection during their login process to the GDPR panel.

  1. In the GDPR section, click the Pen icon on the right to begin the GDPR customisation.

  2. In the first Organisation step, select the Organisation which configuration you would like to use from the dropdown list. Then, click Next in the top right corner to continue to the next step.

  3. In the Login Types step, you can manage the Login Types would be available for selection when logging in to the GDPR panel.

    You can either select all Login Types by selecting All from the toggle button or choose the desired ones in the Specified option.

    After you have specified the Login Types, click Next in the top right corner to proceed.

  4. In the Summary step, you can review the setup. To finish the configuration process, click Save in the top right corner.
