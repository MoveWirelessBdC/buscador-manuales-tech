# Configuring the Service Document Conditions

The Service Document Conditions enable you to differentiate those users who accept the designated Service Documents from those who do not.

1. Add the Condition Node. To learn how to do this, read the [Adding a Condition Node](../adding_condition.html) chapter.
2. In the **Condition Editor**, select the **Service Document**. Then, in the upper right corner, click **Next**.

   ![internet access node creating](../../images/adding_nodes/condition_node_service_document_1.png)
3. In the pop-up box, choose the Service Documents from the dropdown list. Click **Save** to confirm your selection.

   ![internet access node creating](../../images/adding_nodes/condition_node_service_document_2.png)
