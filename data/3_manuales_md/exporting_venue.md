# Export venue

Read this procedure to learn how to export venue settings from the platform.

To export a venue:

1. Go to **Location Management → Venue administration** and then to the **Venue import/export** tab.
2. In the **Venue export** section:

   a. From the **Venue name** list, select the name of the venue you want to export.  
   b. Click **Export**.

   ![export venue](images/export_venue.png)

As a result, the venue has been exported.
