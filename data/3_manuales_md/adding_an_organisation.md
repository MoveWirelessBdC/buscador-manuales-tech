# Creating, Editing and Removing an Organisation

To fully employ the platform's potential, you may want to reflect your company's structure in the system. This allows you to collect and analyse the data of all entities in your organisation. You can reflect your business' structure in the **Organisations** section by creating and accordingly arranging its members.

## Creating an Organisation

To be granted access to the platform, you should first receive the login credentials from the business partner responsible for setting up your account. Then, when you log in to the platform, your organisation should already be created there. Depending on the structure of your business, you may or may not need to create any sub-organisations. However, if your organisation has any subsidiaries which you would like to include in the structure, you can add them as sub-organisations.

There is no limit to the number of organisations and sub-organisations which you may create.

## Adding a Sub-Organisation

1. Go to the **Portal Management → Structure** section and select **Organisations** from the navigation bar.

   **Before you begin creating a sub-organisation, make sure that you switched to the right organisation; it should be highlighted in blue.**
2. Now, click the **Add New Organisation** button to add a sub-organisation.

   ![adding organisation](images/organisations/create_suborg_1.png)
3. In the pop-up box:

   * Into the **Name** field, type the Organisation name (required),
   * From the **Time Zone** dropdown list, select the time zone in which your organisation is located (required),
   * In the **Platform Terms of Use** section, click the **Upload Document** button to upload the Terms of Use which platform users will be required to accept during their first login attempt (optional),
   * From the **Additional Settings** list, select the additional settings (optional, if applicable).
   * Then, click **Save** to confirm the changes.

   ![adding organisation](images/organisations/create_suborg_3.png)
4. A sub-organisation is now created.

   ![adding organisation](images/organisations/create_suborg_4.png)
5. To add another peer sub-organisation, repeat steps 1-4. **Before you proceed, make sure you switch to the desired level of organisation**.

## Switching the Organisation

You may switch the organisation, by clicking on the desired one in the right panel of the **Organisations** tab; such organisation should be highlighted in blue. You may also switch it on the platform top bar. To do this, click the **Organisation** dropdown list, and select the desired organisation. It should be highlighted in blue.

![switch organisation](images/organisations/switch_org_1.png)

![switch organisation](images/organisations/switch_org_2.png)

## Editing an Organisation

1. In the **List of Organisations**, select the organisation which you would like to edit. Then, click the **More** option on the right, and select **Edit**.

   ![edit organisation](images/organisations/edit_org_1.png)
2. Modify the Organisation details and click **Save** to confirm the changes.

## Deleting an Organisation

1. In the **List of Organisations**, select the organisation which you would like to delete. Then, click the **More** option on the right, and select **Remove**.

   ![remove organisation](images/organisations/remove_org_1.png)

   **Note: If you remove an Organisation, you also remove its licences, sub-organisations, users, devices and captive portals. This action cannot be reverted.**
2. In the pop-up box, type the Organisation name into the **Enter the Organisation Name to Confirm** field to confirm deleting the Organisation. Then, click the **I Understand the Consequences, Remove Anyway** button.

   ![remove organisation](images/organisations/remove_org_2.png)
