# Removing access points

In case you've made a mistake in the MAC address of the access point or you're
using a new access point, you may need to remove an access point
from the pane.

To remove access points:

1. Go to **Location Management → Management** and then click the **List of devices** tile.
2. Next to the MAC address of the access point you want to delete, click ![delete button](images/delete_button.png). Click **Confirm**.
