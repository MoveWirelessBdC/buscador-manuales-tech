# Adding, Editing and Deleting Devices

**Devices** (also called Access Points, APs) are essential to manage your WiFi network. For them to work properly, you should first add your devices to the organisation that reflects the venue where these devices are located.

There is no limit to the number of devices which you may add to an organisation.

## Adding a Device to an Organisation

**Note: Before you start, you should ensure that all of your devices have valid licences. To learn how to do this, read the [Checking Device Licences](../../../zzz_unused_files/check_licence.md) chapter.**

1. Go to the **Portal Management → Structure** section and select **Devices** from the navigation bar.

   Note: Make sure that you are in the appropriate organisation. To do this, select the desired one from the right panel or the top bar; it should be highlighted in blue.

   Then, click **+** in the bottom right corner to add a device.

   ![add device](images/devices/add_device_0.png)
2. In the **Add Devices** window, enter the device details:

   * From the **Device Type** dropdown list, select the type of the device,
   * Into the **Device Name** field, type the device name; in most cases, it is a device’s MAC address,
   * In the **IP Address** field ,enter the device’s IP address (recommended),
   * In the **Serial Number** field, enter the device’s serial number,
   * Into the **Descriptive Name** field, type a descriptive name of the device,
   * Into the **MAC** field, enter the device’s MAC address.
     Note: If you insert a MAC address as the device's name in the **AP Name** field, ensure that its MAC address in the **MAC field** is precisely the same.

   Then, click **Next** in the top right corner to confirm the details and proceed further.

   Depending on a selected device type, the pop-up may vary with the range of details which you may be asked to provide. The below list is an example of these details.

   ![add device](images/devices/add_device_2.png)
3. The device is now added to your organisation.

   ![add device](images/devices/add_device_3.png)

   To avoid adding devices one by one, you can import all of them at once. To learn how to do this, read the [Importing and Exporting Devices](import_device.html) chapter.

## Editing a Device

1. From the **List of Devices**, choose the device which you would like to edit. Click the **More** option on the right, and select **Edit** from the dropdown menu.

   ![edit device](images/devices/edit_device_1.png)
2. In the **Edit Device** window, modify the device details, and click **Save** in the top right corner to confirm the changes.

## Deleting a Device

1. From the **List of Devices**, choose the device which you would like to delete. Then, click the **More** option on the right, and select **Remove** from the dropdown menu.

   Note: The device will be permanently removed from the platform and you will not be able to revert this action.

   ![delete device](images/devices/delete_device_1.png)
2. In the pop-up box, click **Remove** to confirm deleting the device.

   ![delete device](images/devices/delete_device_2.png)
