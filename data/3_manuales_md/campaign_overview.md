# Marketing Campaigns Overview

Marketing Campaigns allow you to reach your users with special offers via text messages (SMS) and/or emails that may encourage them to return to your venue. On the platform, you can find five types of Marketing Campaigns: **One-Shot**, **Recurring**, **Birthday**, **Time-Limited**, and **Location-Based**. They differ by the dispatch schedule, which means the campaign sending frequency.

## Campaign Types

### One-Shot Campaign

The **One-Shot** campaign can be used to dispatch a one-time offer to your users.

### Recurring Campaign

The **Recurring** campaign can be used to send users an offer several times in a designated time frame.

### Birthday Campaign

The **Birthday** campaign can be used to send a special offer to a user on their birthday.

**Note: For this kind of campaign to work correctly, the data on the date of birth must be collected during the login process. This may require configuring appropriate login option which gather such data.**

### Time-Limited Campaign

The **Time-Limited** campaign can be dispatched only in a designated time frame. It is sent to all the users that are already in the selected target group, and is continually sent to new users who fall into the targeted audience.

### Location Campaign

Due to configuration differences, the **Location-Based** campaign is described in a separate chapter. To learn more about it, read the [Creating and Managing a Location-Based Campaign](location_based.html) chapter.

## Campaign Editor

All campaigns have dedicated editors which consist of seven steps:

* The **Campaign Overview** step where you can enter the Campaign Name and the Campaign Description,
* The **Target Group** step where you can determine the audience to which you would like to direct the campaign, by creating a desired target group. There is no limit to the number of groups which you can create,

  Note: You may be required to create a Marketing Category which the users should accept during the login process. To learn how to create it, read the [Creating and Managing a Location-Based Campaign](location_based.html) chapter.
* The **Marketing Categories** step where you can select marketing categories by which the recipients should be targeted,

  Note: You may be required to create a Marketing Category which the users should accept during the login process. To learn how to create it, read the [Creating and Managing a Location-Based Campaign](location_based.html) chapter.
* The **Contact Method** step where you can determine how you would like to contact your users: via text message (SMS), email, or both,
* The **Message** step where you can design the content of the message, and send a test message to the designated phone number,
* The **Delivery Schedule** step where you can configure the dispatch schedule (it can be configured in advance),
* The **Summary** step where you can preview the summary of the campaign before saving it.
