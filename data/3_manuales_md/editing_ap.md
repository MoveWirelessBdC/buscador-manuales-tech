# Editing access points position

You may need to modify the placement of access points in a venue. Read this chapter to learn how to do it.

## Deleting an access point

To delete an access point:

1. Go to **Location management→ Management** and then click the **Device manager** tile.
2. Click ![delete device](images/image182.png), and on the venue plan, click the access point you want to delete.
3. Next to the button, click **Save**.

## Moving an access point

Note

You can only move points that are not calibrated on any channel band.

To move an access point:

1. Go to **Location management→ Management** and then click the **Device manager** tile.
2. Click ![move device](images/image186.png), and on the venue plan, grab a marker point of the access point and move it to a new place.
3. Next to the button, click **Save**.

**See also**: [Adding access points](adding_AP_location.html)
