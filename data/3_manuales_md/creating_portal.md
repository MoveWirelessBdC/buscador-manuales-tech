# Creating a Portal

## Creating a Portal in the **Portal Manager**

1. Go to the **User Enrollment → User Journey** section and click the **Portal** option.

   ![create portal](../images/portal/create_portal_1.png)
2. On the **Portal Manager** page, click **+** to create a new Portal in the bottom right corner.

   ![create portal](../images/portal/create_portal_2.png)
3. Type the name of the Portal and select its language. In the upper right corner, click **Next** to confirm your selection.

   ![create portal](../images/portal/create_portal_3.png)
4. On the **Portal Editor** page, design the Portal according to your preferences. To learn how to do this, watch the video below.
5. In the upper right corner, click **Save** to confirm your design changes.

   ![create portal](../images/portal/create_portal_4.png)
6. Before saving, you can preview the Portal for both web and mobile versions. To do this, click **Next** in the upper right corner of the page. If you would like to skip this step, click **Skip and Save** in the upper right corner.

   ![create portal](../images/portal/create_portal_5.png)
7. To create another Portal in the **Portal Manager**, click **+** in the lower right corner and repeat steps 2-6.

   ![create portal](../images/portal/create_portal_6.png)

## Creating a Portal on the **Portals Available** Page

The **Portals Available** page is accessible while creating a Page Flow. To learn how to create a Page Flow read the [Creating and Editing a Page Flow](../page_flow/creating_and_editing_a_page_flow.html) chapter.

1. In the **Page Flow Editor**, add a Portal node to the Page Flow. To learn how to add Portal Nodes, read [Adding a Portal Node](../page_flow/portal_node.html) chapter.

   ![create portal](../images/portal/create_portal_7.png)
2. Type the name of the Portal and select its language. In the upper right corner, click **Next** to confirm.

   ![create portal](../images/portal/create_portal_3.png)
3. On the **Portal Editor** page, design the Portal according to your preferences. To learn how to do this, watch the video below.
4. In the upper right corner, click **Save** to confirm the design changes.

   ![create portal](../images/portal/create_portal_4.png)
5. Before saving, you can preview the Portal for both web and mobile versions. To do this, click **Next** in the upper right corner of the page. If you would like to skip this step, click **Skip and Save** in the upper right corner.

   ![create portal](../images/portal/create_portal_5.png)
6. On the **Portals Available** page, select the Portal. In the upper right corner, click **Select**.

   ![create portal](../images/portal/create_portal_9.png)
