# Suspending and Resuming a Licence

If for some reason, a Licence must be temporarily disabled and prevented from using its allocated assets (AP capacity and data transfer), Representatives may suspend it for an indefinite period, and resume it later on.

## Suspending a Licence

1. First, select the Organisation liable for the desired Licence from the Organisation tree. Then, click the **More** option in the top right corner of the Licence panel, and select **Suspend** from the dropdown list.

   ![suspending sublicence](images/licences/suspend_licence_1.png)
2. In the pop-up box, confirm suspending the Licence along with the consequences of this action.

   ![suspending sublicence](images/licences/suspend_licence_2.png)

## Resuming a Licence

1. Find the Licence which you would like to resume, and click the **More** option in the top right corner of its panel. Then, select **Resume** from the dropdown menu.

   ![suspending sublicence](images/licences/suspend_licence_3.png)
2. In the pop-up box, confirm resuming the Licence, which means that you allow it to continue using its allocated assets.

   ![suspending sublicence](images/licences/suspend_licence_4.png)
