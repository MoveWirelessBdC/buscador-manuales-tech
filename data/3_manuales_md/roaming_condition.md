# Configuring the Roaming User Conditions

The Roaming User Condition allows you to differentiate those users who registered to your WiFi for the first time (the **New** users) from those who logged in to your WiFi once again (the **Returning** ones) in a designated time frame.

1. Add the Condition Node. To learn how to do this, read the [Adding a Condition Node](../adding_condition.html) chapter.
2. In the **Condition Editor**, select the **Service Document**. In the upper right corner, click **Next**.

   ![internet access node creating](../../images/adding_nodes/condition_node_roaming_user_1.png)
3. Now, define the Roaming User by confirming their registration through an additional login attempt, and designate when this second attempt should occur in the pop-up box. Then, click **Save** to confirm the changes.

   ![internet access node creating](../../images/adding_nodes/condition_node_roaming_user_2.png)
