# Editing, Copying, and Deleting a Portal

## Editing a Portal

1. In the **Portal Manager**, click the **More** option and select **Edit** from the dropdown list.

   ![edit portal](../images/portal/edit_portal_1.png)
2. In the **Portal Editor**, edit the Portal according to your preferences. In the upper right corner, click **Save** to confirm your changes.

   Note: If the Portal is already published, you will be able to edit it only to a limited extent.

   ![edit portal](../images/portal/edit_portal_2.png)
3. Before saving, you can preview the Portal for both web and mobile versions. To do this, click **Next** in the upper right corner of the page. If you would like to skip this step, click **Skip and Save** in the upper right corner.

   ![preview portal](../images/portal/create_portal_5.png)

## Copying a Portal

1. In the **Portal Manager**, click the **More** option and select **Copy** from the dropdown list.

   ![copy portal](../images/portal/copy_portal_0.png)
2. Type the name of the copied Portal.

   ![copy portal](../images/portal/copy_portal_1.png)
3. Select one of 3 copying options.

   a. To copy a Portal with no additional elements connected to it, leave the switches turned to **OFF**.

   ![copy portal](../images/portal/copy_portal_2a.png)

   b. To copy a Portal with additional elements connected to it, turn the switch to **ON** in the **Copy Used Elements** option.

   ![copy portal](../images/portal/copy_portal_2b.png)

   c. To copy a Portal as a draft, turn the **Save as Draft** switch to **ON**.

   ![copy portal](../images/portal/copy_portal_2c.png)
4. The copy of the Portal has now been created. You can find it in the **Portal Manager**.

   ![copy portal](../images/portal/copy_portal_3.png)

## Deleting a Portal

1. In the **Portal Manager**, click the **More** option and select **Remove** from the dropdown list.

   ![remove portal](../images/portal/remove_portal_0.png)
2. In the pop-up box, click the **Remove** button to confirm deleting the Portal. If you would like to keep it, click **Keep**.

   ![remove portal](../images/portal/remove_portal_1.png)

Note: It is impossible to remove a Portal which is being used in a Page Flow. To remove it, you must first disconnect the desired Portal from the Flow. Then, follow the steps 1-2. To learn how to disconnect a Portal from a Page Flow, read the [Connecting, Disconnecting and Removing Nodes](../page_flow/connect_node.html) chapter.
