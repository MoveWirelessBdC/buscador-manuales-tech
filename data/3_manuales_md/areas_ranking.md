# Area Ranking

The **Area Ranking** graph allows you to identify the popularity of your venue’s zones. To view these statistics, go to the **Reporting → Behaviour** section and click the **Areas Ranking** tile.

This graph can be displayed for a specified time frame and metric type (visit count, visit length, density). To define it, select the time range in the **Time Picker** located at the top of the Areas window, and choose the metric type from the **Metric Type** dropdown list. Then, click **Show** to confirm your selection.

![area ranking](../images/area_ranking_1.png)

In the top right corner of the venue panel, you can select the floor which you would like to analyse. Look at the legend to identify the most popular areas.

Below the venue panel, you can find a list of the area details. You can filter it, by selecting activity options from the toggle buttons above the list (**Active**, **Inactive**, **All**), or use the search field to find the preferred area.
