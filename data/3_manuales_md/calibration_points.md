# Creating calibration points

Now that you created the calibration you can place the calibration points on your venue plan.

Best practices regarding the placement of calibration points

The placement of calibration points is important for the calibration accuracy and the scanning time.   
The distance between calibration points should be **from 3 to 5 metres** (8 metres distance is also sometimes permissible, but it is not recommended).  
Here are some additional basic requirements for the placement of the calibration points:

* In large rooms, they should be placed in the corners and beside walls.
* In small rooms, one calibration point may be enough; otherwise, they should be placed only in the corners.
* In narrow passageways, they should be placed along the passageway.
* In wide passageways, they should be placed along the passageway, on both its sides.
* Characteristic objects (for example: poles, stands, partition walls) should have additional calibration points placed next to them.

For more details, read **Indoor Location – Best Practices Guide**

To create calibration points:

1. In the **Calibration** panel, click ![add calibration point](images/image156.png) and place the markers of the calibration points on the plan.

   Tip

   Use the **Hide/Show grid** button to, respectively, hide the measuring grid or show it.

   ![add calibration point](images/image164.png)
2. To stop adding calibration points, next to the icon, click **Close**.
