# Adding a Loyalty Program to a Portal

1. Create or enter the editor of an already existing Portal, and from the left menu, select the **Other** category. Then, drag the **Loyalty Program** tile into the Portal workspace.

   ![loyalty programs](../images/loyalty_programs/loyalty_programs_7.png)
2. Next, click on the **Select Loyalty Program** tile on the left menu.

   ![loyalty programs](../images/loyalty_programs/loyalty_programs_8.png)
3. In the **Loyalty Programs Available** window, select the desired Loyalty Program, and click **Select** in the top right corner to add it to the Portal.

   ![loyalty programs](../images/loyalty_programs/loyalty_programs_9.png)
4. You can no customise the appearance of the Program on the left menu. To change the image of stamps, select **Custom** from the **Stamps style** toggle button, and upload the image of your preference.

   **Note:** The maximum size of an image file should be **2MB**, and the suggested dimensions are **44 x 44 px**. You should also use files in **JPG**, **PNG**, **BMP**, or **GIF** format only.

   After you have customised the Loyalty Program's appearance, click **Save** in the bottom right corner to finish adding it to the Portal.

   ![loyalty programs](../images/loyalty_programs/loyalty_programs_10.png)
5. In the **Overview** window, you can preview how the Program looks on desktop and mobile devices. You can also adjust them by clicking the **Go to Mobile/Desktop Editor** buttons at the bottom of the page.

   If you are satisfied with the results, click **Next** in the top right corner to finish saving the Portal.

   ![loyalty programs](../images/loyalty_programs/loyalty_programs_11.png)
6. When the Portal is ready, you should add it to the designated Page Flow to enable users to use the Loyalty Program. To learn how to add a Portal to a Page Flow, read the [Adding a Portal Node](../page_flow/portal_node.html) chapter.
