# Dashboard

The **Dashboard** section provides general information about user behaviour. It consists of two sections, **Users** and **Behaviour**.

![dashboard](images/dashboard/dashboard_0.png)

## Users

![dashboard](images/dashboard/dashboard_1.png)

### Wi-Fi Users

The number of **unique users** logged in today(from midnight in the chosen time zone) compared to the same period yesterday.

### Users Now

The number of **unique users** currently logged in and connected to **W-iFi** compared to the same instant on the previous day.

### Average Connection Duration

The average connection time of all **logged in/connected users** today compared to the same period yesterday.

### Impressions

The total number of passes through the **START** node in the **Flow** compared to the same period yesterday.

![dashboard](images/dashboard/dash_5.png)

### Language

The number of **users** (from midnight in the chosen time zone) per browser detected language.

### User Count over Time

Today's total number of users (including new and returning) in hourly granularity compared to yesterday's data. A returning user is defined as someone who had previously logged in to the WiFi. By default, the last 30 days are taken into account but this option is fully configurable.

### Best Day of the Week

The day with the highest number of unique, logged-in users over the last 7 days.

### Best Day of the Month

The day with the highest number of unique, logged-in users over the last 30 days.

### Best Day on Record

The day with the highest number of unique, logged-in users, ever recorded.

### Sessions Now

The **Sessions Now** section shows detailed information about all active users who are logged in.

![dashboard](images/dashboard/sessions_now.png)

## Behaviour

**Behaviour** is a new dashboard that gives you live information on how users move around your venue. The numbers in this section are updated for today(from midnight in the chosen time zone) and compared to the same moment of the previous day.

![dashboard](images/dashboard/dash_11.png)

### Passers-By Now

**Passers-By Now** are unique users who are found close to your venue but are not classified as spotted inside).
A user who is classified as **close to your venue** is for example someone passing by the building but because of the inaccuracy of the location assessment, the user is picked up as in and out of the venue.
A user who is classified as **spotted inside** is someone who, based on localisation data, is inside the venue for a minimum of 5 minutes.

### Visitors Now

**Visitors Now** are unique users who are classified as spotted inside your **venue**.

### Passers-By Today

**Passers-By Today** are unique users who have been found close to your **venue** but have not been classified as spotted inside.

This number is calculated for today (from midnight to the present) and updated live, and compared to the same moment of the previous day.

### Visitors Today

**Visitors Today** are unique users who have been classified as spotted inside your venue.
This number is calculated for today (from midnight to the present) and updated live, and compared to the same moment of the previous day.

### Passers-By and Visitors

![dashboard](images/dashboard/dashboard_5.png)

Total number of **Passers-By** and **Visitors** calculated for today (from midnight to the present). It is broken per hours and compared with the previous day data.

### New and Returning Visitors

![dashboard](images/dashboard/dashboard_6.png)

Total number of **New and Returning Visitors** calculated for today (from midnight to the present) and updated live. It is broken per hours and compared with the previous day data. To view the specifics for each hour hover your mouse over the appropriate area.

![dashboard](images/dashboard/Dwell_Time.png)

### Average Dwell Time for Visitors

**Average duration of all Visitors** sessions. It is calculated for today (from midnight to the present) and updated live, as a total duration of all **Visitors'** sessions, divided by the total number of sessions

### Total Dwell Time for Visitors

**Total duration of all Visitors** sessions. It is calculated for today (from midnight to the present) and updated live, as the sum of all **Visitors'** sessions.

### Dwell Time Distribution of Visitors

Distribution of the number of Visitors' sessions by their duration. It is calculated for today (from midnight to the present) and updated live.

### Device Mapper

Live Visitors' position at your Venue.

![dashboard](images/dashboard/dash_15.png)

![dashboard](images/dashboard/dash_18.png)

### Traffic Map

The **Traffic Map** table shows live congestion of **Visitors** at your Venue.
You can filter the map’s results by setting the areas and grid. To do this, click the **Filter** icon in the top right corner of the table and select the desired settings.

* **Grid**: Here the **Users per area** for a selected granularity is shown.

![dashboard](images/dashboard/dash_19.png)

* **Areas**: Here you can see two metrics, **count** and **density**.

![dashboard](images/dashboard/dash_20.png)

![dashboard](images/dashboard/dash_21.png)

### User Traffic

The **User Traffic** section applies to counting cameras and is configured separately. This section shows the number of users broken up into who entered and exited the **Venue** and is displayed per hour.

![dashboard](images/dashboard/dash_6.png)

### Traffic per Camera

The **Traffic per Camera** table shows the list of all devices by their **IDs** with the number of entries and exits that have been registered.

![dashboard](images/dashboard/dash_17.png)
