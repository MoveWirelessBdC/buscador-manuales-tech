# Drawing navigation lines

Now that you have marked areas, connected the floors with escalators,
lifts, and stairs, and placed toilets and cashpoints, it is important to
draw navigation lines. Those lines are seen by the customers, so they
can follow them to reach their destination in your venue.

To create a navigation line:

1. In the navigation map, on the left side of the screen, on the taskbar, click ![draw graph](images/draw_area_icon.png).
2. Click on the plan and continue drawing the line.

   Note

   * It is best to draw the lines along the passageways and from the main line draw a line that goes to the middle point of each premises.
   * Draw additional diagonal lines between the lines in the passageways.
   * Remember to connect all the marked places to the line that goes along the passageway, as well as all the stairs, lifts,
     escalators, toilets, and cash machines.

   ![navigation lines](images/image249.png)
3. When you finish drawing the lines, next to the icon ![draw graph](images/draw_area_icon.png), click **Finish**.
4. On the right side of the screen, click **Apply changes**.

As a result, the navigation is created and in the **Premises Management** panel, the marker points next to the name of the premises
have changed from grey to blue.

Note

In order for the customers to see the implemented changes, click **Apply changes** after every change.
