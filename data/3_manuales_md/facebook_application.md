# Facebook Application

1.Login to **developers.facebook.com** using your credentials.

2.Click the create APP button on the right side.

![facebook_app](images/facebook_app/FB_app_0.jpg)

3.Click **consumer** in the next window.

4.Add the details of the application.

![facebook_app](images/facebook_app/FB_app_1.jpg)

5.In the next window choose **Facebook Login**.

![facebook_app](images/facebook_app/FB_app_2.png)

6.Go to **Facebook Login** > **Settings** and provide a valid **Oauth Redirect**:
<https://platform.com/linkyfiFlow/callback/facebook>

![facebook_app](images/facebook_app/FB_app_3.png)

7.Set <https://platform.com/linkyfiFlow/callback/facebook> as a **deauthorized URL** and save changes.

![facebook_app](images/facebook_app/FB_app_4.png)

8.Go to **Advanced** access to request **permission** and **features**.

![facebook_app](images/facebook_app/FB_app_5.png)

9.Choose **permissions** which you need to collect personal data from end users.

![facebook_app](images/facebook_app/FB_app_6.png)

10.Go to the top of the page and switch applications to **live mode**.

![facebook_app](images/facebook_app/FB_app_7.png)

11.Login to the **Platform** and go the **User Enrollment** > **Access Methods** section, and click the **Social Media** tile and click the **+** button in the lower right corner to create a new configuration.

![facebook_app](images/facebook_app/Social_Media_15.png)

![facebook_app](images/facebook_app/Social_Media_022.png)

Note

Depending on which **Social Media** platform is chosen the additional information required may vary.
