# Get started with User Journey

## What is a User Journey and why is it important?

In general, a user journey is a map of steps which reflect users’ interaction with a product. It can be used in 2 ways: **to demonstrate** how the users are currently interacting with your product and **to design** their proper interaction with it.

### Why is it important?

When building a product or an interface, it is essential to create a friendly environment for the users. By friendly, we mean clear, not misleading and, most importantly, well-thought-out. For the more transparent it is, the more actively they will use it.

## Why should you create a User Journey for your users?

The User Journey feature allows you to design the way your users connect with your WiFi. It is **a graphic illustration of a login path**. You can arrange each step and decide what to display on your users’ screens. You can use it just for welcoming purposes but also for marketing ones.

Look at the example of a user journey created in our system.

![sample user journey](images/getting_started_with_user_journey/sample_user_journey.png)

**START** represents the moment a user connects to your WiFi. They are then redirected to the Portal which in this case is a login page. There, they fill in the **Form**. After clicking the **SUBMIT** button, they are given internet access for 1 hour. The Portal automatically redirects them to the Website of your choice, for instance to your company’s site.

It is but an example of what your users’ journey can look like. You can shape it freely according to your needs. For example, you can use the free space on the login page for marketing purposes, gather the data you need from a Form, or give your users access to the Internet for a limited time. You can also use several Portals in the Page flow to extend the contact with end users and to expose them to more content. It’s all up to you.

## How can you use the User Journey in your business?

As already mentioned, you can use it to create a personalised login path, but there are other ways your business can profit from it.

### Monetisation

The Portal is the first level of contact with your users. On the login page, you can not only gather information about the users but also display other media, like advertisements or information about discounts. You can do the same with all of the other Portals which you have created in the Page flow. You can also resell the Portal’s free space to advertisers to display marketing content.

The adverts match the exact target group, so all parties benefit: end users get what they are interested in, advertisers profit from returns on advertising, and your venue benefits from monetising your WiFi instead of only paying for it.

### Loyal customers

The adverts on the Portal will always match the end users. This will make them more eager to come back to your venue for more great offers. They may soon become your loyal customers.
