# Adding, Editing, and Removing Forms from a Portal

## Adding Forms to a Portal

1. In the **Portal Editor**, click the **Login** option in the menu on the left.

   ![add form to portal](../images/forms/add_form_to_portal_1.png)
2. Drag the **Form** tile from the menu on the left and drop it in the Portal workspace.

   ![add form to portal](../images/forms/add_form_to_portal_2.png)
3. In the menu on the left, choose the **Click to Select** option to add an additional Form to the Portal.

   ![add form to portal](../images/forms/add_form_to_portal_3.png)
4. On the **Forms Available** page, select the needed Form. In the upper right corner, click **Select** to confirm you choice.

   ![add form to portal](../images/forms/add_form_to_portal_4.png)
5. The Form has now been added to the Portal.

   ![add form to portal](../images/forms/add_form_to_portal_5.png)

## Editing a Form in a Portal

1. To replace a Form in the Portal, click the **Pen** icon in the **Selected Form** area in the menu on the left.

   ![edit form in portal](../images/forms/replace_form_in_portal_1.png)
2. On the **Forms Available** page, select another Form to add to the Portal. In the upper right corner, click **Select**. To create a new Form, click **+** in the bottom right corner.

   To learn how to create a Form, read the [Creating a Form](creating_questionnaire.html)  chapter.

   ![edit form in portal](../images/forms/replace_form_in_portal_2.png)

## Removing a Form

1. In the **Portal Editor**, click the **More** option in the Form placeholder. Then, select **Remove** from the dropdown menu.

   ![remove form from portal](../images/forms/remove_form_from_portal_1.png)
