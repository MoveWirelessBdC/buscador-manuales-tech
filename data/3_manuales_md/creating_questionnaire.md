# Creating a Form

Forms are created in the **Form Editor** which you can access in two ways:
- through the **Form Manager** page before creating a Portal, or
- through the **Forms Available** page while creating a Portal.

To learn how to create a Portal, read the [Creating a Portal](../portal/creating_portal.html) chapter.

## Creating a Form in the **Form Manager**

1. Go to **User Enrollment → User Journey** and click the **Form** option.

   ![create form in form manager](../images/forms/create_form_manager_0.png)
2. In the **Form Manager**, click the **+** option in the lower right corner to create a new Form.

   ![create form in form manager](../images/forms/create_form_manager_1.png)
3. Type the name of the Form and select its language. In the upper right corner, click **Next** to confirm.

   ![create form in form manager](../images/forms/create_form_manager_2.png)
4. In the **Form Editor**, design the Form according to your preferences. To learn how to do this, watch the video below.
5. In the upper right corner, click **Save** to confirm.

   ![create form in form manager](../images/forms/create_form_editor_1.png)
6. To create another Form in the **Form Manager**, click **+** in the lower right corner and repeat steps 3-5.

   ![create form in form manager](../images/forms/create_form_manager_3.png)

## Creating a Form on the **Forms Available** Page

The **Forms Available** page are accessible while creating a Portal. To learn how to create a Portal, [Creating a Portal](../portal/creating_portal.html) chapter.

1. In the **Portal Editor**, drag the **Forms** component and drop it into the Portal workspace.

   ![create form in forms available](../images/forms/create_form_forms_available_0.png)
2. In the menu on the left, choose the **Click to Select** option to add to the Portal.

   ![create form in forms available](../images/forms/create_form_forms_available_1.png)
3. In the **Forms Available** page , click **+** in the lower right corner to create a new Form.

   ![create form in forms available](../images/forms/create_form_forms_available_2.png)
4. In the **Form Editor**, design a Form according to your needs. To learn how to do this, watch the video below.
5. In the upper right corner, click **Save** to confirm.

   ![create form in forms available](../images/forms/create_form_forms_available_3.png)
6. On the **Forms Available** page, select the needed Form. In the upper right corner, click **Select** to confirm.

   ![create form in forms available](../images/forms/create_form_forms_available_4.png)
