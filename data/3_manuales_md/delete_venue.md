# Deleting a venue

Read this procedure to learn how to delete a venue from the platform.

To delete a venue:

1. Go to **Location Management → Venue administration** and then to the **Advanced options** tab.
2. In the **Venue deletion** section:

   * From the **Venue** list, select the name of the venue you want to delete.
   * Click **Delete** and then to confirm, **Delete it**.
