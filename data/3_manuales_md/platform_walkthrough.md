## Step 3: Captive Portal

When you have set up your business’ structure in the platform, you can begin designing a captive portal, which is a login page that WiFi users must go through to gain internet connection.

To do this, you should create a Portal which would display to your users when they connect to your WiFi. It allows you to choose from various login methods (phone, email, registration, token, captcha, social media), select ads display (images, text, video), define the Portal's visual components (background, text colours, and other), and design it for desktop and mobile.

To learn how to create a Portal, read the following instructions and watch the below video:

* [Creating a Portal](../user_guide/user_enrollment/portal/creating_portal.html)
* [Creating a Multi-Step Login Process](../user_guide/user_enrollment/portal/multistep_login.html)
* [Adding a Portal Desktop Version](../user_guide/user_enrollment/portal/add_desktop_version.html)

**Note:** You can turn on the video captions which may help you fully understand the process.

## Step 4: Forms & Tokens

*The availability of the Tokens feature depends on your licence plan*

To fully benefit from using Portals, you may use Forms or Tokens. Forms are customisable survey components which you can place on a Portal to gather user data, or users’ opinions, according to your preferences. Tokens are vouchers which contain codes that enable you to limit your WiFi to users who hold them.

To learn how to create Forms and generate Tokens, read the following instructions and watch the below video:

* [Creating a Form](../user_guide/user_enrollment/forms/creating_questionnaire.html)
* [Creating and Managing Tokens](../user_guide/user_enrollment/tokens/creating_tokens.html)

**Note:** You can turn on the video captions which may help you fully understand the process.
