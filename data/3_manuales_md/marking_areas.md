# Marking premises on the venue plan

The first step in creating the navigation map is to mark every place, area, to
which the customers may like to be navigated to. For example, in the case of
a shopping mall, you should mark all the shops and shopping stands.

To mark an area on the venue plan:

1. Go to **Location Management → Navigation**.
2. Next to the name of your venue, click **Map**.
3. In the top right corner of the panel, select a floor which you want to divide into areas.
4. On the left, on the taskbar, click ![draw area](images/image161.png).
5. Draw an outline of the area by clicking on the plan. When the outline is finished, next to the icon ![draw area](images/image161.png), click **Finish**.

   ![draw area](images/image239.png)
6. Assign this area to a premises by right-clicking the
   outlined area and selecting ![binding area](images/image227.png) **Bind area to premises** from the list.
7. From the **Available premises** list, select the name of the premises
   that occupies this area and click **Save**.
8. Repeat the procedure for all the premises on the floor and then, for
   all the floors.
9. On the right side of the screen, click **Apply changes**.
