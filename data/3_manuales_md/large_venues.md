# Understanding the Behaviour data from large venues

You can use the behavioural data to:

* Understand consumer visit intent and preferences
* Match digital (and traditional) ads to real-world activity and transactions
* Understand customer activity from an operational perspective (what is the busiest time?) to improve customer experience
* Benchmark store/location performance (internally or competitively)
* Enable more contextually relevant or personalized customer experiences
* Predict earnings and financial performance.

The Location mode, recommended for large venues, provides you with:

* [Location statistics](../../user_guide/reporting/location_mode/statistics.html)
* [Area statistics](../../user_guide/reporting/location_mode/area_stats.html)
* [Heat map](../../user_guide/reporting/location_mode/heat_map.html)
* [Areas ranking](../../user_guide/reporting/location_mode/areas_ranking.html)
* [Flow diagram](../../user_guide/reporting/location_mode/flow_diagram.html)
* [Device mapper](../../user_guide/reporting/location_mode/device_mapper.html)
