# Configuring the Verified User Condition

The **Verified User Conditions** enable you to differentiate those users who verify their email addresses during logins from those who do not.

1. Add the Condition Node. To learn how to do this read the [Adding a Condition Node](../adding_condition.html) chapter.
2. In the **Condition Editor**, select the **Verified User** option. Then, in the upper right corner, click **Next**.
