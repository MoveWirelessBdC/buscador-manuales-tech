# Configuring the Locale Conditions

The **Locale Conditions** enable the creation of different paths, depending on the users’ localisation - which is determined by their browser’s language.

1. Add the Condition node. To learn how to do this, read the [Adding a Condition Node](../adding_condition.html) chapter.
2. In the **Condition Editor**, select **Locale**. Then, in the upper right corner, click **Next**.

   ![internet access node creating](../../images/adding_nodes/condition_node_locale_1.png)
3. In the **Locale Condition Editor**, click **+** in the lower right corner.

   ![internet access node creating](../../images/adding_nodes/condition_node_locale_2.png)
4. In the pop-up box, choose the user’s language from the dropdown list. Click **Save** to confirm.

   ![internet access node creating](../../images/adding_nodes/condition_node_locale_3.png)
