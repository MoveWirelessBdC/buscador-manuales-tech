# Editing an area

You may need to modify the areas created in a venue. Read this chapter to learn
how to do it.

## Editing a shape of an area

To edit the shape of an area:

1. Go to **Location → Management**.
2. From the **Venue** list, select the name of your venue and go to the **Area** tab.
3. Click ![modify](images/image185.png) and modify the needed shape.
4. Next to the button, click **Save**.

## Removing an area

To remove an area:

1. Go to **Location → Management**.
2. From the **Venue** list, select the name of your venue and go to the **Area** tab.
3. To remove an area, click ![delete](images/image182.png) and select the area to be removed.
4. Next to the button, click **Save**.
