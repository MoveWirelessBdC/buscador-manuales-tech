# Flow Diagram

The **Flow Diagram** presents how the users move between the areas which you have defined in your venue. It allows you to analyse the popularity of the zones, make any necessary changes at your facility, as well as plan the placement of ads and the localisation of stores.

To view this diagram, go to the **Reporting → Behaviour** section and click the **Flow Diagram** tile. It can be displayed for a specified time frame, number of steps, and minimal flow size.

To specify these details, select the time range in the **Time Picker** located at the top of the Statistics panel, and type the desired numbers in the **Number of Steps** and the **Minimal Flow Size Filter** fields. Then, click **Show** to confirm your selection.

![flow diagram](../images/flow_diagram_1.png)

To view details of the particular path, hover your mouse over it to see the number of users who used it in a given period.
