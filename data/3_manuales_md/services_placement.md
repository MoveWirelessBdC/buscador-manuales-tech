# Placing cashpoints and toilets

This step is optional. The customers of your venue can be navigated not
only to particular premises but also to cash machines and toilets.

## Placing cashpoints

To place a cashpoint:

1. In the navigation map, on the left side of the screen, on the taskbar, click ![cashpoint](images/image246.png).
2. Place the icon on the plan.
3. On the right side of the screen, click **Apply changes**.

## Placing toilets

To place toilets:

1. In the navigation map, on the left side of the screen, on the taskbar, click ![toilet](images/image247.png).
2. Place the icon on the plan.
3. On the right side of the screen, click **Apply changes**.
