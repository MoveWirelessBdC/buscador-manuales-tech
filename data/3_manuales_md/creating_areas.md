# Dividing the venue into areas (Optional)

It is possible to divide a venue into smaller areas in order to get statistics for each created area and create location-based marketing campaigns.

To divide the venue into areas:

1. Open the **Areas** panel:

   * In the **Device manager** panel, in the top left corner, click the ![back button](images/back.png) and then click the **Areas** tile.
   * Go to **Location → Management** and click the **Areas** tile.
2. In the **Area** panel, in the right upper corner, select the floor
   which you want to divide into areas.
3. Click the ![Mark area](images/image161.png) icon and draw the area:

   a. To start drawing, click on a spot in the plan.  
   b. Continue drawing by clicking on consecutive spots in the plan. When the area is closed, the **Area editor** window opens.
4. In the **Area editor** window, enter the area name and description.

   ![area editor](images/image162.png)
5. Click **Save**.

**What to do next**:

* If you need precise data, then you need to perform calibration. Start with [creating calibration](calibration/creating_calibration.html).
* If you do not want to go through the process of calibration and you need only very rough data, then [configure the engine](calibration/configuring_engine_location.html).
