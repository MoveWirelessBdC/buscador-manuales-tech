# Engagement Statistics

The **Engagement Statistics** give insights into the engagement of the users who log in to your WiFi. For instance, they show how often do they return to your venue and how much time do they spend there.

**Note:** The table loading time depends on several factors, including selected date range, amount of data which must be aggregated, or your internet connection speed. The **average display time** should be **less than 5 min**, and **should not exceed 10 min**. In case it is higher, try reloading the page, or change the date range. If the data is still not displayed, contact your administrator.

To ensure the best system performance, part of the data is stored in probabilistic structures, therefore the expected error in relation to real data can be up to 0.1%.

In most cases, the statistic calculations are based on **Logged-In Users**, which means users who have completed the login process and gained internet access. In other cases, the user type is indicated.

To view these statistics, go to the **Reporting > Statistics** section and select **Engagement** from the upper bar. They can be displayed for a specified time frame; to define it, select the time range in the **Time Picker** located at the top of the Statistics panel, and click **Show** to confirm your selection.

## The **How Much Time Do Users Spend in Your Organisation?** Section

![engagement ststs](images/engagement/engagement_2.png)

### Average User Dwell Time

The **Average User Dwell Time** table presents how long the user sessions lasted. It is calculated as the sum of all sessions’ duration divided by the number of these sessions. The bottom text indicates whether this result is below or above the overall average for the organisation.

### Average Visit Frequency

The **Average Visit Frequency** table displays the average number of days in which the users gained internet access, measured by login days.

### Average User Return Time

The **Average User Return Time** shows the average number of days which have passed between users’ last visits and their returns to the venue. These visits are identified by the user logins and are calculated as the sum of these days divided by the number of users.

### Average Dwell Time per Day

![engagement ststs](images/engagement/engagement_3.png)

The **Average Dwell Time per Day** bar chart presents how long on average the users stayed connected to your WiFi per day. To preview the exact number, hover your mouse over the desired bar.

### Sessions per Dwell Time Range

![engagement ststs](images/engagement/engagement_4.png)

The **Sessions per Dwell Time Range** bar chart displays the number of user sessions broken by the time ranges. To preview the exact number, hover your mouse over the desired bar.

### Average Dwell Time per Organisation

![engagement ststs](images/engagement/engagement_5.png)

The **Average Dwell Time per Organisation** table displays the breakdown of all sub-organisations which have contributed to the organisation’s overall user dwell time.

## The **How Often Do Users Return to Your Organisation?** Section

### Distribution of Users by Login Days

![engagement ststs](images/engagement/engagement_6.png)

The **Distribution of Users by Login Days** bar chart presents the distribution of users broken by the number of days in which they logged in. To preview the exact number, hover your mouse over the desired bar.

## Distribution of Users by Return Time

![engagement ststs](images/engagement/engagement_7.png)

The **Distribution of Users by Return time** bar chart shows the distribution of users broken by the number of days that have passed between their last visit and their return to the venue. To preview the exact number, hover your mouse over the desired bar.

## Exporting the Data

All the graphs can be exported as CSV files. To download them, click the **Export to CSV** icon in the top right corner of the table and save the file on your computer.
