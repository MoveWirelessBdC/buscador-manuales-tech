# Configuring the Time of Day Conditions

The Time of Day Conditions enable the creation of a different Page Flow depending on the specific time when the users choose to log in.

1. Add the Condition Node. To learn how to do this, read the [Adding a Condition Node](../adding_condition.html) chapter.
2. In the **Condition Editor**, select the **Time of Day** option. Then, in the upper right corner, click **Next**.

   ![internet access node creating](../../images/adding_nodes/condition_node_time_of_day_1.png)
3. In the **Time of Day Condition Editor**, click **+** in the lower right corner.

   ![internet access node creating](../../images/adding_nodes/condition_node_time_of_day_2.png)
4. In the pop-up box, define the time range for the conditions. Click **Save** to confirm your changes.

   You can define several time ranges under one condition. To do this, repeat steps 2-4. Then, click **Save**.

   ![internet access node creating](../../images/adding_nodes/condition_node_time_of_day_3.png)
