# Device Mapper

The **Device Mapper** allows you to track devices and observe how they move around your facility in real-time. You can either observe all devices, to analyse their congestion and draw conclusions about your venue’s hot spots, or track an individual one.

To access the **Device Mapper**, go to the **Reporting → Behaviour** section and click the **Device Mapper** tile.

![device mapper](../images/device_mapper_1.png)

To search an individual device, type its MAC Address into the search field located in the upper left corner of the window, or click on a device in the Map. To preview the device’s activity, turn the **Prediction and History** switch **ON**.
