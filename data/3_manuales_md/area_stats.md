# Area Statistics

The **Area Statistics** provide information about user behaviour in the particular areas of your venue. To view these statistics, go to the **Reporting → Behaviour** section and click the **Area Statistics** tile.

The Area Statistics consist of four tabs which you can find at the top of the window: the **Overview**, the **Rush Hour**, the **User Behaviour**, and the **Entrance / Exit**. Some of the tables have already been discussed in the [Location Statistics](statistics.html) chapter. Therefore, this chapter covers only the statistics which have not yet been described.

The below statistics can also be displayed for a specified time frame and granularity (hour, day, or week). To define it, select the time range in the **Time Picker** located at the top of the Statistics panel and choose the granularity from the **Granularity** dropdown list. Then, click **Show** to confirm your selection.

## The Map of Areas

The Map of Areas displays the zones at your venue, and allows you to view the statistics for the selected one. However, you should note that for the Map to work correctly, you must first define those areas in the system.

To select an area, click the desired one on the Map. Then, click **Show Statistics for Area** in the pop-up box.

## Overview

![area stats](../images/area_statistics/area_statistics_2.png)

### Located

Note: The **Located** table has been described in the [Location Statistics](statistics.html) chapter.

### Associated

The **Associated** table displays the number of Associated Users in the designated area.

An **Associated User** is a person whose device has sent at least one request to your WiFi network. The *request* is registered when the user navigates or is redirected to a captive portal, or when they have a mobile app on their device that continuously sends Internet requests.

### Logged-In

The **Logged-In** table displays the number of users who have logged in to your WiFi in the designated area.

### Located Clients

![area stats](../images/area_statistics/area_statistics_3.png)

The **Located Clients** bar chart presents the total number of users who have been located at your venue, per hour. To preview the exact number of users at a specific time, hover your mouse over the desired bar.

## Entrance / Exit

![area stats](../images/area_statistics/area_statistics_6.png)

### Entrance

The **Entrance** table displays the number of users who entered the designated area.

### Exit

The **Exit** table displays the number of users who exited the designated area.

### Best Hour

The **Best Hour** table indicates the time of day when the number of entrances was at its peak.

### Entrance / Exit

![area stats](../images/area_statistics/area_statistics_6a.png)

The **Entrance / Exit** bar chart displays the number of entrances and exits per hour, broken by these two types of activity. To preview the exact number of users at a specific time, hover your mouse over the desired bar.

### Min. and Max. Engagement Type

![area stats](../images/area_statistics/area_statistics_6b.png)

The **Min. and Max. Engagement Type** bar chart presents the number of users distributed by the engagement type: **Window Shoppers**, **Semi-Engaged** and **Engaged**.

The **Window Shoppers** are the users who have been located in the area for up to 3 minutes. The **Semi-Engaged** users are those who have been located in the area for 3 to 10 minutes. At last, the **Engaged** users must have been located in the area for more than 10 minutes.
