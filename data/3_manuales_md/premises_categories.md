# Creating categories of premises

Read this procedure to learn about the creation of categories of premises
in your venue. Later you will be able to assign premises that may
interest customers to these categories.

To create categories of premises:

1. Go to **Location Management → Navigation**.
2. Next to the name of your venue, click **Premises categories**.
3. On the right side of the panel, click **Add category**.
4. In the **Add category** window, type a name of category in the proper language and click **Save**.

   ![add category](images/image226.png)
5. To add more categories, repeat steps 2-4.
