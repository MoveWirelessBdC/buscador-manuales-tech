# Calibrating after editing a venue

If all the access points in your venue were calibrated and you decided to
make changes in your venue structure, you need to repeat the whole
calibration process.

To recalibrate access points after editing a venue:

1. Add a new calibration following the instructions from the
   [creating calibration](creating_calibration.html) chapter.
2. Proceed with calibrating the location following the instructions from the consecutive sections: [creating calibration points](calibration_points.html),
   [configuring access points](configuring_access_points.html), and [gathering calibration data](gathering_data.html).
3. Go to **Location management → Management** and then click the **Engine** tile.

   * From the **Engine Type** list, select the engine used for location.
   * **For advanced users**: Into the **Engine parameters** window, type the appropriate parameters.
   * Click **Save**. As a result, the changes are going to be applied within 10 minutes.
