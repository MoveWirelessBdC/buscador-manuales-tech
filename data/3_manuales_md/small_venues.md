# Get the behavioural data of users in small venues

To get the behavioural data in small venues, you need to configure the location function in the Presence mode.

## Configuring the Presence mode

> What you need:
>
> * Platform account
> * 20 minutes

To configure the Presence mode:

1. [Create a venue](../../user_guide/location_management/presence_mode/creating_venue_presence.html)
2. [Add access points to the plan](../../user_guide/location_management/presence_mode/add_AP_presence.html)
3. [Configure the location engine](../../user_guide/location_management/presence_mode/configure_engine_presence.html)

**See also**: [Understanding the Behaviour data from small venues](small_venues.html)
